Research Areas

Two engines, multiple front doors.

The lab structure is simple: one side studies market signals and execution frameworks, the other studies basketball outcomes and player-level projection systems.

Multi-Asset Signal Research

This side of the lab explores signal generation, validation, ranking, and research tooling for liquid markets and event-driven contracts. It is where the StockArithm and Predeve lines start, along with the B2B market-intelligence face of StockArithm.

NBA Statistical Research
